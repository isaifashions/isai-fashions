Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2024

🛡️ Your privacy is important to us. We are committed to being transparent about how we collect, use, and protect your personal information. Please read this privacy policy carefully to understand our practices.

1. Introduction

Isai Fashions ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") operates the website and services at isai-fashions.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and make purchases.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

We collect information you voluntarily provide when:

  • Creating an order on our website
  • Contacting us via WhatsApp, email, or phone
  • Subscribing to our newsletter
  • Providing feedback or reviews
  • Requesting customer support

Types of Information Collected

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number
  • Shipping Information: Delivery address, city, pincode
  • Payment Information: Payment method (GPay/UPI or COD preference)
  • Order Information: Products ordered, quantities, prices
  • Communication: Messages, inquiries, feedback

Automatically Collected Information

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • IP address
  • Device information

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Order Processing: To process your orders and send order confirmations
  • Delivery: To arrange shipping and deliver your products
  • Communication: To respond to your inquiries and customer support requests
  • Customer Service: To provide customer service and handle returns/exchanges
  • Marketing: To send promotional emails and updates (with your consent)
  • Quality Improvement: To improve our website, products, and services
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with legal obligations
  • Fraud Prevention: To detect and prevent fraudulent transactions

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

When We Share Your Information

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. However, we may share your information:

  • With Courier Partners: To deliver your orders (shipping address, name, phone)
  • For Payment Processing: Only the payment method preference (GPay or COD)
  • Legal Requirement: When required by law or government authorities
  • Business Transfer: In case of merger or acquisition (you'll be notified)

What We Do NOT Share

  • Full payment card details (we don't store these)
  • Passwords or sensitive account information
  • Your personal data with marketers or third-party advertisers
  • Any information beyond what's necessary for order fulfillment

5. Data Security

We take your security seriously. While no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, we implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • We do not store full payment card information
  • Personal data is stored securely locally on your browser
  • Orders are transmitted securely to our WhatsApp
  • We use encryption for sensitive communications
  • Our website is protected against unauthorized access
