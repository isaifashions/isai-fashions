How to Measure Yourself

📏 Measurement Instructions

Bust/Chest

Wrap a soft measuring tape around the fullest part of your bust. Keep the tape snug but not tight. Take the measurement while wearing a regular bra.

Waist

Measure around the narrowest part of your waist. Keep the tape loose and natural. Don't pull it too tight.

Length

For tops: Measure from the shoulder down to where you want the top to end. For kurtis: Measure from shoulder to knee for full length.

Sleeve

From the center of your back, measure to your wrist. Keep your arm slightly bent when measuring.

💡 Sizing Tips

  • Take measurements in the morning when your body is not swollen
  • Wear thin clothing when measuring for accurate results
  • Compare your measurements to our size chart, not the size label
  • If you're between sizes, go for the larger size for comfort
  • Our fabric has minimal stretch - choose based on comfort preference
  • Different styles may fit slightly differently - check product descriptions
  • All measurements are in inches; 1 inch = 2.54 cm
