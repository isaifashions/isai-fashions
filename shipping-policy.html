↩️ Returns & Exchange Policy

Return Window

You have 7 days from delivery to initiate a return or exchange. Items must be unused and in original condition with tags attached.

What Can Be Returned?

  • Items with manufacturing defects (color fading, loose threads, etc.)
  • Items that don't match the description on our website
  • Damaged items received
  • Wrong item delivered

⚠️ Non-Returnable Items

  • Items that have been worn, washed, or damaged by customer
  • Items without original tags or packaging
  • Items showing signs of usage or wear
  • Items purchased on sale or clearance (check product page)

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Contact us within 7 days of delivery via WhatsApp: +91 6381288411
  2. Provide your order ID and reason for return
  3. We'll provide you with return shipping instructions
  4. Pack the item securely with original packaging
  5. Use our provided return label or arrange pickup
  6. Once we receive and inspect the item, we'll process the refund

Refund Timeline

  • Return pickup/delivery: 3-5 days
  • Inspection and processing: 2-3 days
  • Refund to original payment method: 5-7 business days
  • Total: 10-15 business days

💡 Exchange Option

Prefer a different size or color? We offer exchanges! You can exchange for a different size or style within 7 days of delivery, free of shipping charges.

Size Issues

If the item doesn't fit, we can exchange it for a different size absolutely free! Just let us know within 7 days. Please ensure the item is unused and in original condition.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel my order after placing it?

Yes, if the order hasn't been dispatched yet. Contact us immediately via WhatsApp with your order ID. We'll process the cancellation and refund within 2-3 days.

What if my item arrives damaged?

Please unbox and inspect your item immediately. If damaged, take photos and contact us within 24 hours with your order ID and photos. We'll send a replacement or process a full refund.

Do I have to pay for returns?

For defective items, we'll provide a pre-paid return label. For size/style exchanges, return shipping is on us. For change-of-mind returns, return shipping cost may apply.

How do I check my order status?

Check your order dashboard or contact us on WhatsApp with your order ID. We'll provide real-time updates on your shipment.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept GPay (UPI) and Cash on Delivery (COD). Choose your preferred payment method during checkout.

Is my personal information secure?

Yes! We take your privacy seriously. Your information is only used for order fulfillment and will never be shared with third parties. Read our Privacy Policy for more details.
